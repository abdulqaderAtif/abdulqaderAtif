# Hi, I'm Abdulqader 👋

I like understanding how systems work under the hood—and making them my own.

Most of my learning starts with a question, a small experiment, and a willingness to break things. I use **Python**, **Bash**, and **C** to explore ideas, try open-source tools, and understand what happens beneath the surface.

> Customization is freedom.

## What interests me

- **Systems and internals:** following the details behind everyday tools and figuring out why things behave the way they do.
- **Small, hands-on experiments:** testing an idea in code and learning from what happens.
- **Linux and customization:** shaping my desktop, terminal, and editor around the way I work.
- **Open source:** discovering tools, exploring how they're built, and sharing what I learn.
